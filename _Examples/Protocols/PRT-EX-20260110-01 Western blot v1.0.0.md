# Western blot v1.0.0

## Purpose and scope
Detect total p53 and β-actin in cell lysates (training example).

## Safety
- Biosafety level: BSL-1. Acrylamide is neurotoxic: gloves, cast gels in the fume hood.

## Procedure
1. Load 20 µg protein per lane on a 10 % SDS-PAGE gel; run 120 V, 90 min.
2. Wet transfer to PVDF, 100 V, 60 min, 4 °C.
3. Block 5 % milk/TBST 1 h; primary antibody overnight at 4 °C; secondary 1 h.
4. ECL detection; save the **uncropped** image to lab storage and register it.

## Expected results and QC criteria
β-actin band at ~42 kDa in every lane; p53 at ~53 kDa.
